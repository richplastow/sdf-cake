## Problem

The eaten slider cuts one wedge, or, after
[Plan 18](../18-bite-dont-slice.md), one bite where the wedge would be. A
visitor cannot bite where they like.

## Current position

Plan 18 shows one bite and how a bite should look. The slices stay, for
a slice of cake or a cake with a slice gone.

## Recommended fix: a list of bites as uniforms, picked by a click

- A capped list of bites, each a centre, a radius and a seed, as a
  uniform array like the decorations' slots, each one sphere per field
  sample, which the research measured as costing nothing extra and
  keeping the bound; the sphere distorted by the cake's `wonky`, as
  Plan 18 tears its bite, with the seed varying each bite.
- The click's point on the cake is found by a march in JavaScript over a
  port of the field, or by the shader writing the hit distance under the
  pointer to a one-pixel render target read back on click.
- A Bite Mode toggle beside the controls, since a click on the canvas
  today starts a drag.

## Other options

- A fixed set of bite points per recipe, taken in order by the slider,
  no picking needed.

## When to pick it up

After Plan 18 has passed manual QA and the look of a bite is settled.
